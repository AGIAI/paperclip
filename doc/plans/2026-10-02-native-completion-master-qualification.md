## Evidence boundary

The completion case preserves the original assigned-skill prompt, installed marker-only output skill, explicit invocation and independently saved issue document oracle. Production-default hires are verified through the public served bundle before execution. The blocker case preserves the exact owner/action/scope and independently checks durable Blocked status, explanatory visible reply, no additional domain work and no shell/process/file work.

Both require exactly one succeeded native run bound to the issue, complete public events, a matched finishing-tool start/result, actual provider final after that result, no new invocation after proposal, and its persisted run-attributed reply. Authoritative control-plane acceptance/terminal are independent of final timing. Observable ordering does not prove feedback consumption. A single pair cannot establish general coding-quality equivalence. Missing or failed evidence stays explicit.
